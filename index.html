FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Add it to your inquiry and we'll cover it on the call.

What's the difference between the packages?

Essential focuses on great music and exceptional sound. Signature is our complete DJ & MC experience for up to 8 hours, from ceremony to the dance floor. Luxury adds up to 10 hours, premium sound, expanded lighting, and optional atmospheric effects.

Can we add more hours or effects?

Yes. Extended DJ coverage, additional lighting, additional sound coverage, and fog / atmospheric effects are all available as optional enhancements.

Can we choose the music?

Absolutely. Signature and Luxury include personalized music planning, your must-play & do-not-play list, and music requests on the night.

Do you DJ events other than weddings?

Yes. Private parties are custom-quoted. Tell us about your event and we'll put together the right setup.

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have your date. Send an inquiry and we'll confirm availability right away.
